Dear Sir or Madam,

We are pleased to invite you to Monodose Day, hosted by Marchesini Group on 5 October 2026 at its headquarters in Pianoro, Bologna.

During the event, Gualapack sustainable materials will be presented and demonstrated on Marchesini packaging machines, giving you the opportunity to see first-hand how innovative material structures perform in real production conditions.

Why participate?
It’s a unique opportunity to go beyond material samples and technical data: see our solutions running on industrial equipment, evaluate their machinability and performance, and discuss how they can support your transition towards more sustainable, PPWR-oriented packaging.

The day will include:

• Presentation of sustainable materials and packaging solutions
• Live machine demonstrations and production line tour
• Stickpack, sachet and strip packaging technologies
• Direct discussions with Gualapack and Marchesini experts

5 October 2026
Marchesini Group Headquarters
Pianoro, Bologna, Italy

We would be delighted to welcome you in Bologna and explore together the next generation of monodose packaging.
